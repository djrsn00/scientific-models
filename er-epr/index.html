Equations & scientific scope READ

Three distinct levels: a classical BTZ reference geometry, a finite TFD teaching model, and a scripted causal protocol. The protocol does not numerically evolve the Einstein equations or compute a dynamical HRT surface.

Quantum entropy and the TFD construction follow standard quantum mechanics. BTZ is a classical gravitational solution; RT/HRT belong to the semiclassical holographic framework. GJW traversability is a result within specific interacting models.

ER = EPR is a conjectural relationship. AdS/CFT supplies a specific theoretical setting; this simple AdS geometry is not known to describe our universe globally. Generic entangled particles do not imply a visible classical wormhole.

The nonrotating BTZ spatial slice uses r = rh cosh ρ. Its two exterior ends meet at one bifurcation circle. Future horizons and the black-hole interior appear in the causal diagram. The 3D drawing is not an isometric Euclidean embedding.

The gold curve is the short, nonwrapping RT geodesic anchored to region A at the radial cutoff rc/L = 12. In 2+1 bulk dimensions the relevant “area” is a length. The entropy relation is leading semiclassical RT, with a fixed regulator.

The full-left entropy uses the horizon circumference, SL/cCFT = πrh/(3L). The interval value is SA/cCFT = ℓγ/(6L). Neither is the eight-level teaching-model entropy.

The translucent sheet is a spatial slice of the entanglement wedge. Its causal domain of dependence is the full wedge. It represents a reconstruction region rather than material filling the bulk.

The coupling pulse, null-energy cue, causal deformation, and signal timing are schematic. Their sign and causal effect are prescribed to illustrate a GJW-type construction. The background RT curve remains a static reference during the protocol.

Traversability is not modeled by widening a physical bore. The interaction changes causal accessibility in the theoretical construction. A camera flight through the drawing is not a possible causal trajectory.

The protocol provides no entanglement-only or arbitrary faster-than-light communication. Negative averaged null energy is a model-dependent quantum effect, not a macroscopic resource assumed to be freely available.

The teleportation interpretation applies in certain holographic models. Ordinary teleportation and quantum-computer realizations do not establish the creation of gravitational tunnels.
