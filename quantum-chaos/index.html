The matrix residual checks the projected eigenproblem. It is not an error bound for the continuum wavefunction. Higher modes are more sensitive to basis truncation.

A wave packet is projected into the retained modes. Its evolution conserves probability within that finite model. Crossfades between selected states are display transitions.
